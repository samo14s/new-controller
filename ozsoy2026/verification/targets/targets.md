## Ambiguities and inconsistencies

- **A1 Damping ratio.** Table 1 and Sec. 2.2 give 1.45 %; the Fig. 5 identified curve peaks at 2.574e-6 m/N (= 1/(2*0.0145*1.34e7)). But the Fig. 6a uncontrolled peak (-111.10 dB -> 1.339 %), the Fig. 6b open-loop pole (-10.89 +- 812.2j rad/s -> 1.341 %) and the uncontrolled SLD minima (1.212 mm; model with 1.34 %: 1.213 mm) all imply 1.34 %. The computed curves of the paper use 1.34 %.
- **A2 Gain range.** Text: gains between 237 and 1487 V s/m. Fig. 8 maximum 1487 (740 rpm) agrees, but the Fig. 8 minimum is 256.9 V s/m at 1940 rpm. 237 is the single-gain result of Sec. 4.
- **A3 Fig. 6 legend gains.** Legend: 500 rpm g=1487, 1300 rpm g=1162, 2500 rpm g=585, 3000 rpm g=797. Fig. 8 gives 1479 / 1158 / 543 / 794.5 at these speeds (1487 is the Fig. 8 maximum at 740 rpm, not the 500 rpm value). The plotted Fig. 6a peaks and Fig. 6b poles are mutually consistent and, with the 1-DOF + Eq. (3) loop at zeta=1.34 %, correspond to g ~ 1543 / 1162 / 685 / 830. Only the 1300 rpm case is consistent across legend, Fig. 8 and curves.
- **A4 Hidden part of the Fig. 9 controlled SLD.** The controlled curve is one vector path sampled every 10 rpm from 500 to 3000 rpm. Opaque white rectangles drawn after it cover 1500-2050 rpm and 960-970 rpm - exactly the speeds where the controlled curve lies below the uncontrolled one. In the hidden band it falls to 3.23 mm at 1940 rpm while the uncontrolled SLD there is ~29 mm. In print the solid line seems to stop at 1500 and restart at 2050 rpm.
- **A5 Interpolated parts of the gain map.** Fig. 8 has constant-slope stretches 500-550, 560-620, 620-740 (a single segment) and 1410-1860 rpm (25 collinear vertices). These look like linear interpolation rather than per-speed optima; 1410-1860 overlaps the hidden band of Fig. 9.
- **A6 Worst case 1.2 -> 3.6 mm.** 1.2 mm matches the uncontrolled minima (1.212 mm). No 3.6 mm minimum exists on the Fig. 9 controlled curve: the visible minimum is 3.82 mm at 2070 rpm and the hidden one 3.23 mm at 1940 rpm. The Fig. 11 single-gain minima are 3.52 mm.
