RESULT 2 - the box is 4-dimensional, the reachable set is 2-dimensional
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  max |det(D^T D)| over 201 positions = 3.252e-15   (entry scale 3.806)
  -> D^T D is an outer product: rank 1, symmetric, positive semidefinite,
     for every milling position.  One free parameter (x), plus one for
     alpha4.  The reachable set of alpha4 * D^T D is a 2-D surface.

  Eq. (24) instead uses four independent reals delta_DD1..delta_DD4:
     * symmetry broken  : delta_DD2 and delta_DD3 are independent, so
       the box contains non-symmetric 'stiffness' matrices;
     * rank broken      : det is generically non-zero in the box;
     * sign broken      : see the corner scan below.

  indefinite corners present : True

  -> every reachable matrix is semidefinite of ONE sign; the box spends
     part of its volume on matrices of the opposite sign, which no
     milling position can produce.  That volume is pure conservatism,
     and it is paid for in mu.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONSEQUENCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The set used for synthesis is simultaneously
     TOO SMALL  in the direction that matters (Result 1: the
                regenerative stiffness of mode 1 is 39x under-covered),
     TOO LARGE  in directions the physics cannot reach (Result 2).
  Enlarging the box uniformly fixes the first and worsens the second.
  The fix is structural: parameterise the set by the physical variables
  (position x, coefficient alpha4) instead of by four free scalars.
