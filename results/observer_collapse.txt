==============================================================================
SECTION 5.2 - WHY THE OBSERVER IS NOT SCHEDULED
==============================================================================
  perturbation : the reference's +10 % mass / -10 % stiffness box
                 -> the natural frequencies drop by 9.5 %
  evaluated at : x_P = 50.0 mm (mid-edge, the node of
                 the second mode), NO CUTTING

  controller                             max Re(poles) 1/s
  --------------------------------------------------------
  LQG (fixed)                                       -93.62
  K(x_P) only  -- the proposed law                  -93.37
  L(x_P) only                                         2.99   <-- unstable
  K and L together                                    8.47   <-- unstable

  The two variants that schedule the observer are the ones that
  lose the nominal loop.  Scheduling the gain alone stays where
  the fixed design is, to within a fraction of a percent.

  mode shape at mid-edge (design model):
    D(l_P/2) = [6.6518 0.073 ]   -> |D_2|/|D_1| = 1.10e-02
    so E(l_P/2) ~ [0; D_1; 0]: the assumed disturbance carries
    no second-mode content for the scheduled filter to use.
